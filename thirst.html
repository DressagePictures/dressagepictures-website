Thirst

Feature Survival Drama Nonlinear

A big city striver running from a devastating breakup attempts a brutal ultramarathon in the desert. Moving back and forth through time, THIRST follows his incredible survival story as he is forced to face the truth of who he really is—and who he's not.

Qatar Opportunity

For Qatari investors, THIRST is a chance to put Qatari capital behind a story built on the same unforgiving desert terrain that has long shaped the region's own identity — endurance, extremity, and the reckoning that follows. It extends Qatar's growing voice in global cinema at a festival that has built its reputation championing bold, character-driven international storytelling, and it arrives at the exact moment Qatar is positioning itself as a serious production hub: Sony Pictures and NEON have opened regional operations in Doha, and the new Qatar Screen Production Incentive (QSPI) offers a cash rebate of up to 50% on qualifying production spend, with applications opening in Q2 2026.
